Generic Payer Eligibility and Prior Authorization Overview

Prior authorization is required for services designated as "PA Required"
on the payer's procedure code list, currently covering select imaging,
durable medical equipment over $1,000, and non-emergent inpatient
admissions.

Eligibility verification (270/271 transaction) must be completed prior
to submitting a prior authorization request. Requests submitted without
a valid, current eligibility check will be administratively denied and
must be resubmitted.

Appeals: a denied request may be appealed within 30 days of the denial
notice, with new or additional clinical documentation.
