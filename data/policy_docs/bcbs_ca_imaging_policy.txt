BCBS-CA Medical Policy: Advanced Imaging (MRI/CT) Prior Authorization

Section 1: Scope
This policy applies to outpatient MRI and CT imaging procedures billed
under CPT codes 70000-76499 for members enrolled in BCBS-CA commercial
and Medicare Advantage plans.

Section 2: Clinical Criteria for MRI Brain (CPT 70551-70553)
Prior authorization for MRI Brain will be approved when ONE of the
following is met:
  a) New focal neurological deficit with onset within 90 days, OR
  b) Suspected stroke or TIA with non-diagnostic CT, OR
  c) Chronic headache (ICD-10 G43.x, R51) with at least one red-flag
     feature: onset after age 50, sudden "thunderclap" onset, or
     abnormal neurological exam.
Routine imaging for uncomplicated primary headache without red-flag
features does not meet criteria and requires peer-to-peer review.

Section 3: Documentation Requirements
Requests must include: clinical notes from the ordering provider dated
within 60 days, relevant prior imaging reports if available, and
documentation of any conservative treatment attempted.

Section 4: Turnaround
Standard requests: 5 business days. Urgent requests with documented
clinical urgency: 72 hours.
